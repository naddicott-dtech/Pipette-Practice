# Follow-up — 2026-05-07: GelBox visibility, layout, descent feel, trash anchor

After C4 shipped, hands-on testing surfaced four regressions / missing
landmarks. This is the implementation summary; the design plan is at
`/root/.claude/plans/enchanted-fluttering-hedgehog.md`.

## The four issues

1. **Wells are visually invisible** outside of LOAD_WELL — the dark well
   bodies blended into the dark slab when nothing was highlighting them.
2. **Layout doesn't match a real gel chamber.** Wells were on the front
   edge with the polarity axis on Z; user wants wells on the right edge
   with `−` at the well end and `+` at the far (left) end.
3. **LOAD_WELL descent is too fast** (full descent in 1.5 s) and there
   was no visual landmark for the buffer surface, well rim, or agar
   floor — players couldn't gauge depth at all.
4. **DISCARD_TIP eject anchored the pipette inside the table.** Tip apex
   was at world Y = -1.2 with the beaker bottom at Y = 0.05 — apex was
   1.25 units below the floor.

## Changes

### Persistent well visibility — Bug 1

`src/components/GelBox.tsx`:
- Added a persistent gold rim ring at every well opening, regardless of
  step. The well now reads as a well at every step.
- Removed `transparent: true` from the well body unless the well is
  ghosted as a past lane — eliminates the depth-test occlusion against
  the transparent slab/buffer.
- Lane label (`Well N`) now shows whenever the well is empty, not just
  during LOAD_WELL.

### Right-side layout + polarity axis swap — Bug 2

`src/scene/targets.ts`:
- `WELLS` now line up along Z at constant `WELL_X = 6` (right edge of the
  buffer chamber, whose right edge sits at world x=7). Z range:
  -1.8…1.8 with `WELL_SPACING = 1.2`.

`src/components/GelBox.tsx`:
- Polarity labels: `−` at world (x=7, z=0), `+` at world (x=-1, z=0).
  Both rotated to lay flat on the slab so they read from OVERVIEW.
- Bands now migrate along -X (right → left), not -Z. `Band` component's
  position prop swapped from `[0, -0.05, -pos]` to `[-pos, -0.05, 0]`.
- Well box geometry swapped: was `[0.8, 0.2, 0.4]` (long on X), now
  `[0.4, 0.2, 0.8]` (long on Z).
- DNA fill block geometry swapped from `[0.7, 0.1, 0.3]` to
  `[0.3, 0.1, 0.7]` to match.
- `GEL_ORIGIN` moved from `[3, 0, -1]` to `[3, 0, 0]` so well-axis math
  reads cleanly.
- "ELECTROPHORESIS CHAMBER" title left in place at the front edge.

`src/scene/targets.test.ts`:
- "wells sorted by x and non-overlapping" replaced with "wells line up
  along z, non-overlapping along z".

### Descent timing + visual landmarks — Bug 3

`src/sim/config.ts`:
| Constant | Old | New | Why |
|---|---|---|---|
| `Y_DESCENT_START` | 1.4 | 1.0 | Just above the buffer surface (y=0.25) |
| `Y_DESCENT_PUNCTURE` | -0.1 | -0.25 | Visibly below the well floor (-0.05) |
| `HIGH_TO_GOOD_MS` | 600 | 1800 | t=0.6 → tip Y at buffer surface |
| `GOOD_TO_PUNCTURE_MS` | 1100 | 2520 | t=0.84 → tip Y at well floor |
| `AUTO_PUNCTURE_MS` | 1500 | 3000 | 2× slower; good zone window 720 ms |

The numbers are chosen so timing thresholds line up with *visible*
surfaces: buffer surface at HIGH→GOOD, well floor at GOOD→PUNCTURE.

`src/components/GelBox.tsx` adds two depth-gauging visuals:
- **Buffer surface band** — a thin emissive cyan slab at the water line
  (world y=0.25), sized short of the well column so it doesn't occlude
  the wells from above. Players see the tip cross "into the water".
- **Descent-zone rim recolor** — the active well's rim recolors live
  while phase === 'descending':
  - cyan when `descentMs < HIGH_TO_GOOD_MS` (still descending)
  - green when in the good zone — this is the "press now" cue
  - red when past `GOOD_TO_PUNCTURE_MS`

Combined with the slower descent, the player has three independent
landmarks (surface line, well rim, zone color) to gauge depth.

### Trash anchor — Bug 4

`src/sim/config.ts` — added `PIPETTE.Y_LOWERED_TRASH = 2.5`. With body
center at Y=2.5 and tip apex 2.0 below body center, apex lands at
Y=0.5 — mid-beaker (beaker walls span Y=0.05 to Y=1.25).

`src/components/Pipette.tsx` — `anchorYFor` `case 'trash'` returns
`PIPETTE.Y_LOWERED_TRASH` instead of `PIPETTE.Y_LOWERED_TIPS`.

`src/scene/Trash.tsx` — exported `BEAKER_HEIGHT`, `BEAKER_RADIUS`,
`BEAKER_FLOOR_Y` so the config invariant test can assert the geometry
relationship.

`src/sim/config.test.ts` — new invariant: with body at
`Y_LOWERED_TRASH`, the tip apex must be above `BEAKER_FLOOR_Y` and
below `BEAKER_FLOOR_Y + BEAKER_HEIGHT`. The test pins the
"poke through the bottom" regression.
